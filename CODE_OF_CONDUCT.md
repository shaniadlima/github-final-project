# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our project a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include being respectful, accepting constructive criticism, and focusing on what is best for the community. Harassment, insults, and abusive behavior are not acceptable.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including repositories, discussions, and other project-related activities.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive behavior may be reported to the project maintainers, who will review the report and take appropriate action.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers will review each incident fairly and may issue a warning, remove inappropriate content, or take other appropriate corrective action.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, a community Code of Conduct.
